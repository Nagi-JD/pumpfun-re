`err_at!` note: three steps (s7, s9, s10) use a local `macro_rules! err_at` placed at the start of lib.rs line 1, so no line shifts. It expands exactly as Anchor's `error!` does, but takes the filename and line as literals. This keeps the error variant and the runtime behaviour, but the origin it reports comes from the deployed site, not from where the call sits in our file. It is a measurement device for "this closure exists in that caller"; the source-faithful form is line placement in the right file.
The err_at steps account for 5 of the 47 gained windows (s7 +2, s9 +2, s10 +1). The line-placement-only state was measured only up to s6, at 753; s8 sits on top of s7, so a pure-placement total beyond 753 has not been measured.
